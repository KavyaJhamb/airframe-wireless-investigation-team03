Evidence and limits

Read from headers

    Not provable from headers

    • Why the authentication server is silent: down, unreachable from the controller, or misconfigured. The frames only show that the APs never get an answer.
    • Anything a client sends when no sensor can hear it. Attempts that start with the AP's reply are partial views.
    • Packet loss at the receiver. A retry bit means the sender retransmitted; it does not say the frame was lost.
    • Business impact. The captures contain no payloads or layer-3 traffic.
